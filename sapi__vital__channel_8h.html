Detailed Description

Safety-critical redundant channel abstraction with voting logic.

Wraps multiple underlying IPC channels with voting/arbitration logic for safety-critical (vital) channels. Implements 2oo2, 2oo3, and NMR voting strategies with automatic fault detection and safe-state handling.
